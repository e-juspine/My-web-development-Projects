Admissions

Everything you need to know before applying

Admission Requirements

  • GCE Advanced Level (2 papers) or an equivalent diploma
  • Certified copies of academic certificates
  • Birth certificate and national identity card
  • Four passport-size photographs
  • Application fee receipt

Application Process

  • Step 1: Choose your program
  • Step 2: Fill in the online application form below
  • Step 3: Submit your supporting documents at the registry
  • Step 4: Attend the entrance interview
  • Step 5: Receive your admission letter and register
