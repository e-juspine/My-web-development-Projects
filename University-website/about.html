About Our University

Founded in 2001 • Quality Education, Experienced Lecturers, Practical Training, Career Opportunities, International Certifications

Our History

Trustech University Institute Of Buea was created in 2001 as a vocational I.T Centre to train individuals on basic computer skills. Today the institution welcomes more than 400 students in eight departments and 35 accredited programs.

Over the years the university has built modern laboratories, a digital library and partnerships with companies that host our students for internships every academic year.

Who We Are

A community of students, lecturers and researchers working for a better society.

Our Mission

To provide quality, affordable and practical higher education that prepares students for the professional world and for lifelong learning.

Our Vision

To become a leading university recognised for academic excellence, innovation and responsible citizenship.
