Privacy Policy

Template document — review with a qualified professional and adapt to local data-protection law before publishing.

Information collected

When you submit the appointment request form, the following details are collected: name, email address, phone number, preferred date and time, requested service, consultation method and any message you choose to add.

How information is used

  • To respond to and confirm appointment requests.
  • To arrange and deliver the nursing care requested.
  • To keep necessary records in line with professional obligations.

No automatic transmission

This website currently has no connected backend or booking service. Form data is validated in your browser and is not transmitted or stored on a server until such a service is connected.

Sensitive health information

Please do not submit detailed medical information through this website. Clinical details are discussed privately by phone or in person.

Sharing

Personal information is never sold. It is shared only where required to provide care or where legally obliged.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time using the contact details on the website.

Contact

For any privacy question, contact example@email.com.
